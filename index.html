English Level Diagnostic Assessment

This assessment helps us determine your current English proficiency (A1–C2) so we can recommend the best starting point for your learning plan. Results are reviewed with an academic consultant.

Estimated time
45–60 minutes
Sections
Reading • Listening • Writing
Speaking
Scheduled interview (face-to-face)
Start Assessment →

Please do not use translators or dictionaries during the assessment. If you need access credentials, contact your academic consultant.

What happens after you finish

  • Your responses are securely sent to an academic consultant for review.
  • An academic consultant contacts you to schedule a short interview.
  • Your final level and placement recommendation are discussed in that consultation.

Recommended setup

  • Use headphones for the listening section.
  • Take the test in one sitting, in a quiet place.
  • Mobile is supported, but a laptop is ideal.
