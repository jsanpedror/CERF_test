Scoring (0–5)

Criterion Score Anchor descriptors (quick)
Fluency
speed, pausing, flow
0 — No functional output / cannot sustain even basic responses.
1 — Very slow, frequent long pauses; often breaks down.
2 — Short bursts; heavy hesitation; needs time to formulate.
3 — Generally smooth for familiar topics; noticeable hesitation but meaning stays clear.
4 — Mostly natural pace; occasional searching; pauses rarely disrupt.
5 — Natural flow; hesitation minimal and strategic; sustains extended talk.
Accuracy
grammar control
0 — Output not assessable / cannot form meaningful sentences.
1 — Frequent errors; meaning often unclear; relies on single words.
2 — Many errors; meaning usually recoverable; limited control of basic forms.
3 — Errors present but meaning clear; basic structures mostly controlled.
4 — Good control; errors occasional and rarely cause misunderstanding.
5 — Strong control with minor slips; can self-correct effectively.
Range
vocabulary + structures
0 — No functional range evident.
1 — Very limited; repetitive words; cannot paraphrase.
2 — Basic range for immediate needs; struggles beyond simple topics.
3 — Adequate for familiar topics; some paraphrasing; limited complexity.
4 — Flexible range; varies structures; some precision in meaning.
5 — Wide, precise range; uses complex structures appropriately and naturally.
Pronunciation
intelligibility
0 — Speech not intelligible / cannot be assessed.
1 — Frequently hard to understand; listener must guess often.
2 — Understandable with effort; frequent strain (sounds/stress).
3 — Generally intelligible; occasional issues cause confusion.
4 — Clear overall; minor issues don’t affect meaning.
5 — Very clear; natural stress/intonation supports meaning; easy to follow.
Interaction
turn-taking, repair, comprehension
0 — Cannot interact meaningfully.
1 — Needs constant support; cannot manage basic turn-taking.
2 — Answers simple questions; limited ability to ask/clarify.
3 — Maintains exchange; can ask for clarification; some repair strategies.
4 — Handles interaction smoothly; adapts; repairs breakdowns well.
5 — Highly responsive; manages nuance and negotiation naturally.
Coherence
organization, linking
0 — No coherent message; output not assessable.
1 — Disconnected words/phrases; no clear sequencing.
2 — Simple sentences; limited connectors; sometimes hard to follow.
3 — Basic organization; simple linkers; generally clear.
4 — Clear structure; range of connectors; develops points.
5 — Well-structured discourse; effective markers; argues/qualifies/concludes.
