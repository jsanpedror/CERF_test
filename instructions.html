Global Communication Assessment Instructions
Candidate: —

Before you begin

This diagnostic assessment evaluates your current English proficiency (A1–C2). Your results are reviewed by an academic consultant and discussed during a scheduled interview. Results are not displayed on-screen.

Assessment rules

  • Complete the test in one sitting (45–60 minutes).
  • Please do not use translators, dictionaries, or AI tools.
  • Choose the best answer even if you are not fully sure.
  • Some items will increase in difficulty as you progress.

I confirm that I will complete the assessment independently, without translators or assistance.

Sections

  • Reading (adaptive)
  • Listening (adaptive)
  • Writing (short response)
  • Speaking (face-to-face interview, scheduled)

After you finish

  • Your responses are sent to an academic consultant for review.
  • An academic consultant will contact you to schedule an interview.
  • Your final placement recommendation is discussed in that consultation.
Placement Disclaimer

This diagnostic assessment is designed exclusively for pedagogical placement purposes within Global Communication programs.

Results obtained do not constitute an official certification of English language proficiency and should not be interpreted as equivalent to standardized examinations (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge English).

The assessment provides an estimated CEFR-aligned placement level intended to guide instructional decisions and course entry points.
