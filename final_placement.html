Writing mini-rubric (0–5)

Criterion Score Anchor descriptors (quick)
Task achievement
addresses prompt, detail
0 — Not assessable / off task.
1 — Minimal response; largely incomplete.
2 — Partially addresses task; limited detail.
3 — Addresses task; adequate detail for level.
4 — Fully addresses task; relevant elaboration.
5 — Strong fulfillment; precise, well-developed response.
Coherence
organization, linking
0 — Not assessable / no coherent message.
1 — Disconnected phrases; hard to follow.
2 — Simple sequencing; limited connectors.
3 — Clear basic structure; simple linking (and/but/because).
4 — Clear paragraphs; varied connectors; logical flow.
5 — Strong structure; discourse markers used effectively.
Accuracy
grammar control
0 — Not assessable.
1 — Frequent errors block meaning.
2 — Many errors; meaning usually recoverable.
3 — Errors present; meaning clear; basic forms mostly controlled.
4 — Good control; errors occasional and non-disruptive.
5 — Strong control with minor slips; can self-correct (if edited).
Range
vocabulary + structures
0 — Not assessable.
1 — Very limited; repetitive; cannot paraphrase.
2 — Basic range for immediate needs; limited variety.
3 — Adequate for familiar topics; some paraphrasing.
4 — Flexible range; more precise word choice; some complexity.
5 — Wide, precise range; complex structures used appropriately.
